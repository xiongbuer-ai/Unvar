# Brand and icons

English | [简体中文](../zh/brand.md)

The Unvar mark combines an open U with a separate dot at the upper right. The U suggests a container for research material; the dot represents a clue or unnamed variable waiting to be tested.

## Usage

Use charcoal (`#242424`) and white (`#FFFFFF`) and preserve the original proportions. The recommended minimum display size is 16 px, with clear space of at least one eighth of the mark's width on each side. Do not stretch the mark or add shadows or gradients. Use **Unvar** as the accompanying name; the Chinese name is **未命名变量**.
